      How it works

      Discover

      Every publicly trusted certificate is logged in Certificate Transparency. Cert Radar queries crt.sh for the domain and all subdomains, merges precertificate duplicates, and groups renewals of the same name set into one lineage.

      Classify

      A lineage looks manual when it comes from a commercial CA with no ACME history, runs close to the old 398-day maximum, or renews on an irregular rhythm. Let's Encrypt, ZeroSSL, Google Trust Services, Amazon and Cloudflare certs count as automated. These are heuristics; the reasons are shown on every row.

      Forecast

      Each manual lineage is projected under the 200, 100 and 47-day caps, assuming renewal about a week before expiry. Unclear lineages count as half. Live checks hand-shake each host to confirm which certificate is actually served.

      Schedule: CA/Browser Forum Ballot SC-081v3 (April 2025). 200 days from March 15, 2026; 100 days from March 15, 2027; 47 days from March 15, 2029. Domain validation reuse drops to 10 days in 2029.
